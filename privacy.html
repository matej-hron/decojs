Privacy Policy

How decotheory.eu, the DecoTheory mobile app and the optional dive logbook handle your data

Short version

The theory pages, quizzes, sandbox and the mobile app keep everything locally on your device. The one exception is the optional dive logbook (invite-only, a hobby project): if you log in there, what you save is stored on a server in the EU. No analytics, no advertising, and your data is never sold.

Website (decotheory.eu)

  • Uses browser localStorage to remember your dive setups, quiz progress, and language preference. This data never leaves your browser.
  • A service worker caches the site's HTML, CSS, and JavaScript so it works offline. The cache lives only in your browser; you can clear it at any time via your browser's settings.
  • No cookies are set.
  • External links (e.g. to Wikipedia, GitHub, Strany potápěčské) are loaded only when you click them, never automatically.

DecoTheory mobile app

  • Uses AsyncStorage to remember your diver profile, dive plans, and quiz progress. This data is stored only on your device.
  • Requires no Android permissions beyond what Expo SDK 54 includes by default for app installation.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
  • The decotheory:// deep-link scheme is used only for sharing plans between the app and this website. No data is sent to any server.

Dive logbook (optional, invite-only)

The logbook is a personal hobby project, not a company or a commercial service. It is offered to invited friends as is, without guarantees.

  • Who runs it: Matěj Hron, a private person. Contact: matej.hron@gmail.com.
  • What is stored when you use it: your email address (and the name and email of your Google account if you sign in with Google), the dives and dive computer recordings you upload, the log entries you write, photos you add, dive sites with their positions, buddy names you type in, and your profile.
  • Where: in a Supabase database and file storage in the EU (Frankfurt). The static site itself is hosted by GitHub Pages. Google sign-in is handled by Google. Maps and place search send the search text, the map area or a site position, and your IP address to Mapy.com (and, as a fallback, to OpenStreetMap). Exporting loads a zip library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com.
  • Why: to keep your personal dive logbook. Your data is yours. Other invited members see your dives (with the dive stories you write for them), photos, buddy names and profile (name, avatar, country) unless you mark a dive Private; they never see your notes or your email, and see a site’s exact position and its info link unless you untick Show the exact location for that dive (it is on by default; you can change the default in your profile).
  • Public links: if you turn on the public link of a dive, anyone who has that link can see that one dive without an account: the profile and analysis, date, depth, time, gas, dive story, site name, photos, video links, buddy and guide names, and your display name and avatar. Never your notes, your email or your other dives; the site’s exact position unless you unticked it; otherwise only its approximate area, rounded to about 1 km. Pages behind a link are not indexed by search engines. Turning the link off stops it at once; a photo address someone already opened can keep working for up to 10 minutes.
  • Qualifications and medical checks: if you add them in your profile, they are stored with their scans in a private storage area only you can open. Medical checks are health data: they are never shown to other members, in the feed or through public links. Of a qualification, members see only the agency and level, and only if you tick it for that card. Photos are scaled down and their location data removed before upload.
  • How long: until you delete it. It is not used for anything else.
  • Your rights (GDPR): for access and a copy, use Export in the ⋯ menu to download everything as a zip. To correct something, edit your entries. To delete, use Delete all my data in the same menu: it removes all your dives, recordings, photos and sites, your qualifications and medical checks with their scans, and empties your profile. Removing the login itself needs me, so email matej.hron@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Czechia: Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz).
  • No tracking or advertising cookies. Logging in keeps a session token in your browser's local storage.

What we explicitly do NOT do

  • No analytics or telemetry of any kind.
  • No crash reporting service.
  • No advertising.
  • No user accounts on the website or in the mobile app; only the optional dive logbook has a login.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No server that sees your activity, except the dive logbook when you use it.
  • Your data is never sold or shared for advertising.
