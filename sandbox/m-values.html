Where do a and b come from?

ZH-L16A's a and b aren't fitted to data — they're computed from half-time alone. Variants B and C are post-hoc adjustments to specific compartments.

a(t½) = 2 · t½−1/3  (bar)
b(t½) = 1.005 − t½−1/2
