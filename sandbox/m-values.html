Coefficients of all compartments

Compartments are mathematical models of saturation rates, not specific anatomical tissues. The examples are only approximate analogies.

Compartment Approximate analogy ZH-L16A ZH-L16B ZH-L16C

Variants B and C mostly change coefficient a, shifting the M-line. Coefficient b, which affects its slope, stays the same for TC2–16. TC1 is the exception: variants B and C use a different half-time, so b changes too.

Where do a and b come from?

ZH-L16A's a and b are computed from half-time. Variants B and C adjust selected compartments.

a(t1/2) = 2 · t1/2−1/3 bar
b(t1/2) = … − t1/2−1/2
