    🧭 How the Algorithm Builds a Schedule

    From one tissue state to a complete decompression profile, one decision at a time

    Change the dive and watch every decision move

    The charts below run the same Bühlmann ZH-L16C code as the Sandbox. They compare the conventional staged schedule with two educational alternatives.

    The four decisions

    1

    Load the tissues

    Schreiner handles descent; Haldane handles time at depth.

    2

    Try a direct ascent

    The simulated surface state is checked against GF High.

    3

    Find the GF Low anchor

    Only a failed direct ascent creates a decompression anchor.

    4

    Clear each level

    Wait, test the next shallower level, then move or wait again.

    Three profiles, one tissue model

    The modes differ in scheduling convention, not in Bühlmann tissue kinetics.

    Where the minutes go

    Standard mode emits every active 3 m level; adaptive mode records only required waiting.

    Why three modes?

    Mode Grid Minimum stop Purpose
    Standard staged 3 m / 1 min 1 minute at every active level Conventional profile, compatible with Decotengu
    Adaptive staged 3 m / 1 min None Shows mathematically necessary waiting only
    Fine grid 0.1 m / 0.1 min None Visualizes the near-continuous ascent boundary
    Study modes are explanatory tools. They do not produce a conventional operational schedule and must not be used for a real dive.
